The heart of the place

The table you're sitting at was made by a trainee.

Lumberjack is part of London Reclaimed, an employment charity running paid traineeships for young people from South East London.

Have a look around. The tables, the chairs and the shelves were all made by London Reclaimed's joinery trainees. And the café's profits go straight back into training.

So your coffee is good, and it does some good too. That's what we mean by coffee, craftsmanship, community.
